# Промпты иллюстраций Gapchi AI

Созданы встроенным imagegen из соответствующих макетов кабинета. Прозрачность включена при генерации; после сохранения проверен настоящий альфа-канал. Промпты генерации:

## empty-leads

Use case: precise-object-edit / background-extraction. The reference is a Gapchi AI dashboard screenshot. Extract and faithfully recreate ONLY the small decorative clipboard illustration in the middle of the empty leads table. It is a dark navy/slate blue upright clipboard, light blue clip on top, simple person silhouette and two short abstract horizontal lines on its face, with a soft coral organic shape to its left and small coral/slate dots to the right. Keep the soft polished 2.5D illustration style, proportions, coral #FF7277 and muted blue palette. Remove ALL dashboard, cards, borders, letters, words, numbers and surrounding background. Output a single standalone clean cutout asset on a genuinely transparent background with alpha, not a checkerboard drawing. Center the illustration, tightly but comfortably framed with approximately 10% transparent padding. No white rectangle, no navy rectangle, no caption. Keep the object and decorative shapes; no new symbols or extra decorations. This asset will be used in a real dark and light themed website.

## empty-conversations

Use case: precise-object-edit / background-extraction. The reference is a Gapchi AI inbox screenshot. Extract and faithfully recreate ONLY the decorative pair of overlapping speech bubbles in the center of the main empty conversation pane. Front-left coral #FF7277 rounded chat bubble with tail, three lighter dots inside. Back-right muted slate blue rounded chat bubble with tail, three muted light blue dots. A small coral dot above and one slate blue dot lower right, as in the reference. Soft polished 2.5D illustration with subtle volume and gentle shadows, restrained rather than shiny plastic. Remove ALL dashboard, text, buttons, typography, card, large rectangular background and UI. Output one clean standalone illustration on a genuinely transparent background with real alpha, not checkerboard pixels. Center, close framing with about 10% transparent padding, no text, no white or navy rectangle. Preserve recognizable shapes and relative arrangement from the reference. Suitable as a web empty-state image on dark and light surfaces.

## onboarding-conversations

Use case: precise-object-edit / background-extraction. Reference is a Gapchi AI overview screenshot. Extract and faithfully recreate ONLY the decorative illustration at the RIGHT of the upper onboarding banner: a small dark navy/slate window outline with three tiny coral/yellow/blue dots at top, a wide light slate speech bubble near upper left with two short abstract lines, and a smaller coral rounded speech bubble lower right with two short light lines. Preserve its layered composition, softened edges, subtle volume and coral #FF7277 / muted slate palette. Include the small decorative coral rays and muted organic shapes belonging to that group, but remove the entire dashboard, banner, card rectangles, headings, text, numbers, buttons and all background. The small window itself is part of the illustration and must remain. Output ONE standalone illustration on genuine transparent alpha background. Center and fill the frame with about 10 percent transparent padding. No letters or words, no checkerboard pixels, no full-canvas rectangle. It will sit in a real website onboarding card.

## empty-inbox

Use case: precise-object-edit / background-extraction. Reference is a Gapchi AI overview screenshot. Extract and faithfully recreate ONLY the small muted blue EMPTY INBOX TRAY illustration in the "Последние обращения" card near the lower right of the dashboard. It is a compact slate blue open inbox tray with a dark upper opening, rounded lower front and three small muted blue attention rays above it. Preserve its simple polished 2.5D style, subdued blue palette and soft edges. Do not include any letters, numbers, heading, text, zero badge, card, buttons, chat bubbles or other UI. Output one clean isolated standalone illustration on a genuinely transparent background with alpha, not painted checkerboard. Centered close framing, about 10 percent transparent padding. No white rectangle, no navy background rectangle. This is an illustration asset to reuse for empty recent requests and an empty inbox list.
